Evaluation Framework

Key Assessment Pillars

Our independent lab inspection covers both physical hardware resilience and cloud telemetry fidelity.

Hardware & Physical Durability

Thermal resistance, IP water/dust sealing, vibration tolerance, internal battery reserve, and harness integrity under automotive conditions.

Crash & Impact Telemetry

High-frequency 3-axis accelerometer logging, rollover detection, severe impact alert latency, and pre-crash buffer capture.

Location Precision & CAN-Bus

Multi-constellation GNSS accuracy (GPS/GLONASS/Galileo), odometer precision, fuel economy data, and engine diagnostic reporting.

Data Security & Anti-Tamper

TLS 1.3 transmission encryption, authenticated firmware signing, SIM card lock, and instant alerts when device is disconnected.

End-to-End Pathway

Evaluation & Certification Process

How telematics hardware transitions from public inquiry to an accredited MIROS certificate.

01

Public Inquiry

Vendor submits company, contact, and device model details via the public intake form.

02

Registration

Operations Manager reviews inquiry, issues formal invoice, and provisions vendor access.

03

Lab Scheduling

Payment verification completed; inspection slot scheduled with certified MIROS assessors.

04

Evaluation

Assessors conduct hardware inspections, CAN-bus audits, and calculate weighted criteria scores.

05

Certification

Final approval by MIROS Manager, issuing official Star Rating certificate & audit PDF.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Who needs MIROS telematics assessment?

Any telematics vendor, vehicle tracking supplier, fleet management solution provider, or automotive manufacturer operating in Malaysia that requires compliance verification for commercial transport, insurance, or safety accreditation.

How do I register my company for evaluation?

Simply visit our public intake page (Want to register your company? Click here) and fill in your organization details. A MIROS Operations Manager will review your inquiry within 24 hours.

How long does the testing process take?

Once payment is verified and hardware samples are delivered to the laboratory, evaluations are typically completed within 5 to 7 business days.

Is my data kept confidential?

Yes. All vendor documentation, telemetry logs, and test results are stored in an encrypted database with strict multi-tenant access control and certified auditor governance.
