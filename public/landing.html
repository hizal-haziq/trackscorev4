Evaluation Pathway

How It Works

A clear four-step process for evaluating commercial vehicle tracking systems.

1

Apply

Submit an assessment application with company information and vehicle tracking hardware details.

2

Assessment

MIROS evaluates the tracking system against defined technical criteria and functional capabilities.

3

Rating

An objective 1 to 5 star rating is determined based on verified basic and additional features.

4

Results

Official assessment results and verified certificate information are recorded and can be viewed.
