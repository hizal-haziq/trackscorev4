Platform Specifications

The MIROS 5-Star Telematics Standard

Devices are benchmarked across hardware durability, crash event precision, and data encryption into an official star grade recognised by transport regulators and commercial insurers.

★★★★★ 5-Star Elite Grade 90% – 100%

Full CAN-bus telemetry, precision crash g-force profiling, real-time failover, and hardware-level encryption.

★★★★☆ 4-Star Advanced 80% – 89%

Reliable sensor telemetry, anti-tamper triggers, 4G cellular backup, and verified OBD diagnostic compliance.

★★★☆☆ 3-Star Standard 70% – 79%

Core location tracking, speed monitoring, and standard fleet telematics reporting meeting basic regulatory thresholds.

★★☆☆☆ 2-Star Conditional 50% – 69%

Entry-level GPS tracking with conditional compliance; recommendations provided for hardware upgrade.

Key Assessment Pillars

Independent laboratory evaluation covering physical hardware resilience, sensor fidelity, and data security.

Hardware & Physical Durability

Thermal resistance, IP water/dust sealing, vibration tolerance, internal battery reserve, and harness integrity under automotive conditions.

Crash & Impact Telemetry

High-frequency 3-axis accelerometer logging, rollover detection, severe impact alert latency, and pre-crash buffer capture.

Location Precision & CAN-Bus

Multi-constellation GNSS accuracy (GPS/GLONASS/Galileo), odometer precision, fuel economy data, and engine diagnostic reporting.

Data Security & Anti-Tamper

TLS 1.3 transmission encryption, authenticated firmware signing, SIM card lock, and instant alerts when device is disconnected.

Evaluation & Certification Process

End-to-end pathway from initial inquiry submission to official MIROS accredited certification.

01 • Public Inquiry

Vendor submits company, contact, and device model details via the public intake form.

02 • Registration & Invoicing

Operations Manager reviews inquiry, issues formal invoice, and provisions vendor access credentials.

03 • Lab Scheduling

Payment verification completed; inspection slot scheduled with certified MIROS assessors.

04 • Technical Evaluation

Assessors conduct hardware inspections, CAN-bus audits, and calculate weighted criteria scores.

05 • Certification

Final approval by MIROS Manager, issuing official Star Rating certificate & audit PDF.
